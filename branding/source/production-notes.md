## Print

- Finished flat trim: 297 × 210 mm (A4). PDF media: 315 × 228 mm, including crop-mark space.
- 3 mm bleed beyond trim; PDF TrimBox and BleedBox are embedded.
- Outside left-to-right: 97 mm tuck-in flap / 100 mm back / 100 mm front.

- Gold ticks outside trim indicate folds. Grey crop marks indicate trim. Marks are not part of the finished piece.
