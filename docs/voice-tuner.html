Voice Tuner

Tune how a Piper voice speaks (speed, expressiveness, rhythm) and how it sounds (clarity, warmth, air, noise cleaner, volume). Your values are saved in this browser for each voice. Export them and send the file to apply them as that voice’s defaults in the app.

Voice and text

Tuning

Piper length scale. 1.0 is the voice’s normal pace.

Piper noise scale. Lower values can reduce wobble; very low can sound flat.

Piper noise width. Changes how sound lengths vary.

Sound (applied after the voice speaks)

Clarity, warmth, air and volume are in dB; the cleaner is a strength in %. All start at 0 (no change).

Save and share

Tuning is saved automatically in this browser. Export creates a file with every voice you tuned.
