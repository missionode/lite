# Self-Exploration / Quiet Courage

## Approved scope

Add a section named **Self-Exploration** directly below Journey Preparation. It is visible only while Advanced Features is unlocked. Quiet Courage is the first optional activity in that section; two future games may be added later, but are not part of this implementation.

## Experience and ordering

- Quiet Courage may be selected alone without selecting a chakra, or combined with a chakra journey.
- Combined route order: existing Journey Preparation activities, including Guided Noting, then Quiet Courage, then chakra stages.
- Provide 3-, 5-, and 8-minute choices; default to 5 minutes.
- The guided exercise supports private, optional reflection. The participant need not speak, disclose an answer, or take real-world action.
- Begin with a low-level 396 Hz Root-associated symbolic grounding cue, not a therapeutic claim. Follow the active Drone Duration profile exactly (4/10/14/20 seconds, including its fade-in/out); suppress when No Frequency is enabled.
- Locking Advanced Features hides the section, clears the selection and blocks stale/direct launch.
- Add localized UI, narration and roadmap keys for English, Malayalam, Hindi, Russian and Tamil.
- Keep narration gender-neutral in every supported language; avoid assigning the meditator a gender.
- Load the practice module only when selected and precache it for offline use.

## Review and verification

Implementation is local and uncommitted. Focused unit/source tests, locale JSON parsing and syntax checks are the acceptance evidence. Browser layout, mobile rendering, and actual voice playback remain for owner review; do not claim those verified.
