## Meditation Made Understandable

This beginner-friendly programme introduces meditation gradually through breath, attention, bodily awareness, visualization, sound, and the traditional seven-chakra contemplative framework.

Participants experience:

- Breath awareness and Box Breathing

- Noting and Labeling
- Seven-chakra guided meditation

- Resonant Energy Meditation
- A concluding mindful music experience

## The Nine-Day Journey

1. **Root:** meditation basics, breathwork, grounding, stability, safety, and belonging.
2. **Sacral:** creativity, emotion, adaptability, healthy pleasure, personal boundaries, visualization, and an introduction to the Vijnana Bhairava Tantra.
3. **Solar Plexus:** focused attention, confidence, agency, and disciplined action without harsh self-judgement.
4. **Heart:** compassion, gratitude, emotional balance, healthy boundaries, and Ho'oponopono-inspired reflection.
5. **Throat:** authentic expression, mindful communication, deep listening, and Undo & Unlearn.
6. **Third Eye:** body awareness, observation, interpretation, intuition, and Guided Body Scan.
7. **Crown:** meaning, connection, humility, spacious awareness, Guided Noting, and all seven chakras.
8. **Integrated Sound Experience:** two appropriate meditation add-ons, all seven chakras, and optional sound-therapy-inspired meditation.
9. **Resonant Energy Meditation:** uplifting sound, focused attention, mantra-inspired vocal energy, mindful listening, a music jam, and a sustainable personal practice plan.
