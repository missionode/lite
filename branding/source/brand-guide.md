# Visual Direction

## Positioning

Premium, immersive, beginner-friendly, technologically supported meditation. The environment should feel private and exclusive rather than like a crowded cinema.

## Hero composition

- Exactly two spacious, gently inclined premium seats.
- Exactly two calm participants, viewed from behind.
- A large screen showing an infinite, realistic cosmos.
- Dim architectural lighting with restrained warm highlights.
- No religious symbols, medical imagery, visible UI, or crowd.

## Palette

- Midnight: `#050B1A`
- Deep blue: `#081A36`
- Srishti blue: `#0865C7`
- Cosmic violet: `#7459CE`
- Champagne gold: `#D7AE69`
- Soft white: `#F4F6FB`
- Muted text: `#B9C4D8`

## Typography

Use a clean geometric sans serif with generous spacing. Headings should be concise and premium. Body text must remain highly legible over opaque or near-opaque panels.

## Formats

- Print: A4 landscape, two-sided tri-fold, CMYK conversion to be handled by the selected printer if required.
- Digital: portrait PDF suitable for WhatsApp, email, and website sharing.

## Public wording

Use **Resonant Energy Meditation** instead of HRIM in general marketing. Describe sound therapy as optional and sound-therapy-inspired. Do not state or imply medical outcomes.
