Chakra Training-Need Assessment

Ask each question conversationally and answer from how life has generally felt during the past two weeks. Choose the response that fits best, even when it is not the green balanced response. Use “it varies” whenever more context is needed.

This is a wellness and meditation reflection tool, not a clinical diagnostic or emergency assessment. Its results describe the client’s current self-report and should guide conversation, not label the person. Google translation requires an internet connection.
