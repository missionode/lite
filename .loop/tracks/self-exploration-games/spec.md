# Self-Exploration challenges

Status: owner-approved for the bounded, non-explicit version described below. Local implementation only until release review.

## Goal and placement

Add three optional Advanced Features practices to the existing Self-Exploration section, after Quiet Courage in the journey plan and before chakra stages. All may run alone, alongside other preparation choices, or be skipped/stopped using the existing journey controls.

1. **Confidence Visualization** — a guided, non-explicit, fully clothed self-image/body-appreciation visualization. No nudity, body inspection, touching, idealized appearance, or pressure to change or act.
2. **Deep Secrets** — optional spoken self-expression. The guide invites a general thought, wish, feeling, or imagined story; the meditator may speak, use fiction/metaphor, remain silent, skip, or stop. Localized narration uses first-person phrasing (“I won’t record or save what you say here”) in English, Malayalam, Hindi, Russian and Tamil. Technically, the PWA does not request microphone access or capture, transcribe, store or upload speech. Do not imply confidentiality from an in-room listener. No probing, identifying details, scoring, profiling, upsell or real-world action.
3. **Final Challenge** (RETIRED 2026-10-06: the optional-service offer is no longer part of any journey; it is a standalone developer-mode Lobby card, hidden for a care-first client) — a brief checklist card followed by a calm five-second countdown and one optional information preference: “Would you like to learn about the optional intimate service?” Buttons: Yes, No, Skip for now. No response is preselected. Answer is transient only: no persistence, analytics, assessment, operator record or automatic service activation. Yes only tells the meditator they may ask the guide for information after the practice; no/skip continue without comment.

## Safeguards and acceptance

- Never reward disclosure, willingness, or a Yes response. Countdown has no flashing, urgent audio, deadline penalty or blocked exit; stop/skip remains available throughout.
- Keep the service question informational and optional; do not turn it into sexual content, treatment advice or automatic upsell.
- Use the standard translation bundles for UI and spoken guidance in English, Malayalam, Hindi, Russian and Tamil.
- Include selected durations in estimates and roadmap, lazily load selected modules, precache required offline assets, and synchronize the executable flow atlas.
- Test stage order, stand-alone eligibility, Advanced Features lock, loading, countdown cancellation, every answer path, no-persistence, localization coverage, skip/stop cleanup, service-worker cache and atlas.
- No browser/device audio evidence unless explicitly requested. No production publication until a release review reconciles the current dirty branch with production.
