# Deep Secrets — guided spoken self-expression

Status: owner-approved and implemented locally as part of `.loop/tracks/self-exploration-games/spec.md`. See that track for current acceptance criteria and verification evidence.

## Intent

Explore an optional, adult-oriented self-expression practice in the Advanced Features → Self-Exploration area. Unlike Quiet Courage, the core activity is speaking rather than imagining or writing. The meditator may privately speak about a thought, desire, wish, imagined story, or words they usually hold back. They may use fiction or metaphor, remain silent, pass, pause, or stop.

## Implemented experience

1. **Set expectations:** Explain that participation is optional, the app does not record or save speech, and no answer or disclosure is required. Make the listening context clear: a guide or other person who is physically present may hear the meditator.
2. **Invite expression:** Offer a brief, open prompt to speak in their own words, with no examples that steer or bias the content. Do not ask for names or identifying details.
3. **Leave unhurried silence:** Narration pauses while the meditator chooses whether to speak. Do not enable microphone capture, transcription, uploads, or background recording.
4. **Close the exercise:** Gently signal the end, reorient to breath and room, and affirm that speaking, silence, or stopping are all complete choices. Do not ask the meditator to act on anything they described.
5. **Optional debrief:** After the guided practice, the meditator may share how speaking felt. Never prompt them to repeat the secret, story, or intimate detail; declining needs no explanation.

## Safety, privacy and product boundaries

- No game score, streak, reward, unlock, or completion condition may depend on revealing more.
- No operator probing, assessment/profile inference, intimate-service upsell, or reuse of disclosures.
- The experience is not private from an in-room guide or other listener. Do not promise confidentiality beyond the app's actual behavior.
- No microphone permission is needed for the proposed version; the app must neither capture nor store the spoken content.
- Avoid explicit sexual instructions, pressure to undress, non-consensual scenarios, or prompts to perform an imagined act in real life.
- Preserve a visible/available stop route and the participant's ability to keep eyes open or return to grounding.

## Placement and implementation record

Implemented as the second new Self-Exploration item, after Confidence Visualization. Its UI, spoken prompts, localization, route and safeguards are covered by `.loop/tracks/self-exploration-games/spec.md`; the executable atlas now records the live behavior.

## Current status

The feature is implemented locally and remains unpublished pending full validation/release integration. Speech is spoken in the room but is never captured, transcribed, stored or uploaded by the app. Browser/device narration playback has not been tested.
