# Petal reference — review copy (step 1)

Source: Arthur Avalon (Sir John Woodroffe), *The Serpent Power*, 1919, pp. 116, 138–139, 144 (public domain; [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/x-the-serpent-power)). Data: `data/petals.json`.

**Order:** Petals are counted from the right, going round the lotus (dakṣiṇāvarta), letter 1 on petal 1. The texts list letters and qualities in order; pairing them one to one by that order is the usual convention, not stated outright.

**Framing:** These qualities describe tendencies of the mind traditionally associated with each centre, offered for reflection, not as diagnosis or claims about health.

## Root · Mūlādhāra — 4 petals

Location: Base of the spine · Hand rests: Lower back, at the base of the spine

| # | Letter | Attribute | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | वं vaṁ | Paramānanda | Supreme bliss |
| 2 | शं śaṁ | Sahajānanda | Natural bliss |
| 3 | षं ṣaṁ | Yogānanda | Bliss of union (yoga) |
| 4 | सं saṁ | Vīrānanda | Bliss of the courageous |

*The four petals are four forms of bliss (Tarkālaṅkāra’s commentary on the Mahānirvāṇa Tantra). Woodroffe’s table lists the last two the other way round (Vīrānanda, then Yogānanda).*

## Sacral · Svādhiṣṭhāna — 6 petals

Location: Lower belly · Hand rests: Lower belly

| # | Letter | Attribute | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | बं baṁ | Praśraya | Credulity |
| 2 | भं bhaṁ | Aviśvāsa | Suspicion |
| 3 | मं maṁ | Avajñā | Disdain |
| 4 | यं yaṁ | Mūrcchā | Delusion (or disinclination) |
| 5 | रं raṁ | Sarvanāśa | False knowledge |
| 6 | लं laṁ | Krūratā | Pitilessness |

*Order from the Adhyātmaviveka as quoted in Woodroffe’s text. His table gives a different order: Praśraya, Krūratā, Sarvanāśa, Mūrcchā, Avajñā, Aviśvāsa.*

## Throat · Viśuddha — 16 petals

Location: Throat · Hand rests: Collarbones, just below the throat

| # | Letter | Attribute | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | अं aṁ | Praṇava | Om |
| 2 | आं āṁ | Udgītha | Om as chanted in the Sāma Veda |
| 3 | इं iṁ | Huṃphaṭ | A protective seed sound |
| 4 | ईं īṁ | Vaṣaṭ | An offering call |
| 5 | उं uṁ | Svadhā | Offering to the ancestors |
| 6 | ऊं ūṁ | Svāhā | Offering into the fire |
| 7 | ऋं ṛṁ | Namaḥ | Salutation |
| 8 | ॠं ṝṁ | Amṛta | Nectar |
| 9 | ऌं ḷṁ | Ṣaḍja | Musical note sa |
| 10 | ॡं ḹṁ | Ṛṣabha | Musical note ri |
| 11 | एं eṁ | Gāndhāra | Musical note ga |
| 12 | ऐं aiṁ | Madhyama | Musical note ma |
| 13 | ओं oṁ | Pañcama | Musical note pa |
| 14 | औं auṁ | Dhaivata | Musical note dha |
| 15 | अं aṁ | Niṣāda | Musical note ni |
| 16 | अः aḥ | Viṣa | Poison |

*Order from Woodroffe’s table (the only complete list of sixteen). His text instead places poison on the 8th petal and nectar on the 16th. Nectar and poison both came from the churning of the ocean: the upbuilding and destructive forces of the world.*

## Third Eye · Ājñā — 2 petals

Location: Between the eyebrows · Hand rests: A fingertip between the eyebrows

| # | Letter | Attribute | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | हं haṁ | Guṇas | Sattva, rajas and tamas made manifest (shared) |
| 2 | क्षं kṣaṁ | Guṇas | Sattva, rajas and tamas made manifest (shared) |

*Woodroffe gives the three guṇas (sattva, rajas, tamas) across the two petals and the centre, without a per-petal split; the sacred syllable Om sits in the centre.*

## Crown · Sahasrāra — 1,000 petals

Location: Top of the head · Hand rests: Top of the head

*The fifty letters, a to la (the second La, or Kṣa in another reading, is left out), repeated twenty times make the thousand petals, read from beginning to end going round the lotus. No separate quality is given to each petal.*

## Total

50 lettered petals + 1,000 Crown petals = **1,050**.
